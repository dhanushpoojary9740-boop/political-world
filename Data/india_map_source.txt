This boundary file was supplied by the project user. The feature properties contain mixed boundary-vintage labels (including 2011_c and 2019). It is a community-maintained geographic reference, not an authoritative Survey of India boundary product. Political parties, government control, election outcomes, and administrative performance displayed on the map are generated by the game's fictional simulation and are not real-world election or government records.

The game uses this file offline at res://data/india.geojson. Do not rename it or move it out of the data folder.
